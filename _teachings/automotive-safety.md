## Course Overview

This course addresses the safety needs of the human–vehicle–road system. Students will study the fundamental theories of automotive safety, learn protection methods and design applications, and develop interdisciplinary analytical skills. Course content includes:

- Fundamental concepts and frameworks of automotive safety
- Classical methods and their recent developments
- Specialized research topics in automotive safety
- Interdisciplinary research involving vehicle engineering, numerical computation, artificial intelligence, and data analysis

## Prerequisites

- Calculus
- University Physics
- Mechanics of Materials
- Basic knowledge of vehicle theory and control theory is recommended

## Textbooks

- Jianqiang Wang, Bingbing Nie, and Hong Wang, *Intelligent Automotive Safety*, China Communications Press, 2022
- Azim Eskandarian, ed., *Handbook of Intelligent Vehicles*, 2012
- Yoganandan, ed., *Accidental Injury: Biomechanics and Prevention*, 3rd ed., 2015
- Hang Li, *Statistical Learning Methods*, 2019
- King, *The Biomechanics of Impact Injury*, 2018
- Simms et al., *Pedestrian and Cyclist Impact: A Biomechanical Perspective*, 2009
