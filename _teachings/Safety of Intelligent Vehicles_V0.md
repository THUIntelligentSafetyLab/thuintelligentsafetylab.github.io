## Course Overview

This course introduces the fundamental theories, research topics, and future trends of intelligent vehicle safety. Through this course, students are expected to understand how vehicle safety technologies affect human life, build a knowledge framework for future transportation safety, appreciate the value of science, technology, and industry, and develop the courage and ability to create and take responsibility.

Course content includes:

- Fundamental theories, including an overview of automotive safety and occupant restraint systems, etc
- Research hotspots, including intelligent vehicle safety protection and thermal runaway protection for electric vehicles
- Future trends, including intelligent vehicles, future mobility, and transportation safety
