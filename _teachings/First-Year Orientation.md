## Course Overview

This one-credit course consists of 16 hours across eight sessions. It is conducted in a small-class format and combines classroom instruction with experiential learning, interactive discussion, feedback, and student presentations. Classroom and discussion-based activities account for approximately 10 hours, while practical activities account for approximately 6 hours.

The course is organized into three connected modules:

- **Me and My University:** Students explore the characteristics of university study and life, learn about Tsinghua's history, traditions, and culture, and build supportive relationships with teachers and peers.
- **Me and My Major:** Using intelligent vehicles as an accessible interdisciplinary case, students examine technological development, experience research and industry practice, and learn the basic process of scientific inquiry.
- **Me and Myself:** Students reflect on their interests, strengths, values, and goals, strengthen their resilience and emotional adjustment, and consider the responsibilities that accompany scientific and technological development.

The course includes classroom teaching, activities in natural or social environments, laboratory and industry visits, small-group discussions, practical exercises, and feedback. Exact guest speakers, visit sites, and activity arrangements may vary by year. Current course announcements take precedence.
