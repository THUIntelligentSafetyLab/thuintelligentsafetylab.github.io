Safety of Intelligent Vehicles

This undergraduate general education elective examines the evolution of automotive safety from passive protection to intelligent safety. It introduces fundamental theories, research methods, current research topics, and future directions in intelligent vehicle and mobility safety.

Instructor: Bingbing Nie, XXX

Term: Fall

Location: Room XXX, Xth Teaching Building

Time: Period X on XX, Weeks 1–XX

Course Overview

This course introduces the fundamental theories, research topics, and future trends of intelligent vehicle safety. Through this course, students are expected to understand how vehicle safety technologies affect human life, build a knowledge framework for future transportation safety, appreciate the value of science, technology, and industry, and develop the courage and ability to create and take responsibility.

Course content includes:

  • Fundamental theories, including an overview of automotive safety and occupant restraint systems, etc
  • Research hotspots, including intelligent vehicle safety protection and thermal runaway protection for electric vehicles
  • Future trends, including intelligent vehicles, future mobility, and transportation safety

Grading

  • Midterm assessment: 40%
    • A research report completed during the semester
    • One or two in-class quizzes
    • Course experiment reports and related work
  • Final assessment: 50%
    • A safety-related design project
  • Attendance and participation in classroom discussions: 10%
  • Bonus credit: up to 10 additional points

Schedule

Week Date Topic Materials
1 Introduction

Course information; road traffic and intelligent vehicles; the meanings of intelligent vehicle safety; and the research subjects, methods, trends, and classic cases of automotive safety.
2 Overview of Automotive Safety

An introduction to active and passive safety through specific vehicle safety issues.
3 Occupant Restraint Systems

Basic concepts of automotive occupant restraint systems, simplified analysis of the collision process, and restraint system design principles.
4 Research Methods in Automotive Safety

Experimental methods, numerical methods, human surrogates, and a discussion of active occupant response.
5 Field Study: Mobility on the Production Line

An off-campus field study, tentatively planned in Yizhuang with Luobo Chuxing, Baidu, or Qingzhou Chuxing.
6 Behavioral Characteristics of Traffic Participants

An introduction to diverse road users and intelligent cockpits, with a discussion of travel preferences.
7 Electric Vehicle Safety

A guest lecture on the characteristics and safety categories of electric vehicles, as well as battery crash safety and protection.
8 Thermal Runaway Protection for Electric Vehicles

A case-based discussion covering electric vehicles and their accident characteristics, thermal runaway protection for traction batteries, and risk prediction associated with battery damage.
9 Operational Safety of Intelligent Vehicles

Operational safety theory, risk identification and quantitative assessment, and safety decision-making and control.
10 Intelligent Vehicle System Safety

Fundamental concepts of intelligent vehicles, functional safety, safety of the intended functionality, information security, and a discussion of the relationship between artificial intelligence and intelligent vehicles.
11 Automotive Safety Testing and Evaluation

Test scenarios and tools, regulations and consumer-oriented assessment methods, and a discussion of safety testing in the context of ongoing transformations in the automotive industry.
12 Experiential Laboratory Class

Immersive interaction in hazardous traffic scenarios.
13 Automotive Safety and Future Mobility

A seminar exploring future safety under intelligent connectivity and vehicle-road coordination, the development of intelligent protection, and the relationship among intelligent vehicles, smart transportation, and future cities.
14 Automotive Safety and Future Society

A seminar examining whether road traffic crashes will always exist and the ethical dilemmas underlying autonomous vehicle decision-making.
15 Course Project Presentation

Group presentations and instructor feedback on the course projects.
