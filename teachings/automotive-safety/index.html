Automotive Safety

This course provides a comprehensive introduction to the scientific foundations, research methods, and emerging technologies of automotive safety. It covers interdisciplinary topics including vehicle engineering, numerical computation, artificial intelligence, and data analysis, with an emphasis on vehicle engineering.

Instructor: Bingbing Nie, Qing Zhou

Term: Fall

Location: Room 6B106, Sixth Teaching Building

Time: Period 2 on Tuesdays, Weeks 1–11

Course Overview

This course addresses the safety needs of the human–vehicle–road system. Students will study the fundamental theories of automotive safety, learn protection methods and design applications, and develop interdisciplinary analytical skills. Course content includes:

  • Fundamental concepts and frameworks of automotive safety
  • Classical methods and their recent developments
  • Specialized research topics in automotive safety
  • Interdisciplinary research involving vehicle engineering, numerical computation, artificial intelligence, and data analysis

Prerequisites

  • Calculus
  • University Physics
  • Mechanics of Materials
  • Basic knowledge of vehicle theory and control theory is recommended

Textbooks

  • Jianqiang Wang, Bingbing Nie, and Hong Wang, Intelligent Automotive Safety, China Communications Press, 2022
  • Azim Eskandarian, ed., Handbook of Intelligent Vehicles, 2012
  • Yoganandan, ed., Accidental Injury: Biomechanics and Prevention, 3rd ed., 2015
  • Hang Li, Statistical Learning Methods, 2019
  • King, The Biomechanics of Impact Injury, 2018
  • Simms et al., Pedestrian and Cyclist Impact: A Biomechanical Perspective, 2009

Schedule

Week Date Topic Materials
1 Introduction

Course information, the current state of road traffic safety, and the research subjects and methods of automotive safety.
2 Fundamental Concepts and Technologies of Automotive Safety

An introduction to active and passive automotive safety technologies through events related to automotive safety.
3 Occupant Restraint Systems

Fundamental concepts of occupant restraint systems, occupant collision dynamics, restraint system design principles, recent research on intelligent vehicle safety technologies, and future trends in automotive safety.
4 System Safety, Operational Safety, and Intelligent Protection

Fundamental concepts and guiding principles, safety decision-making for intelligent vehicles, system safety, operational safety, and intelligent protection.
5 Dimensional Analysis, Experimental Techniques, and Numerical Methods

Dimensional analysis, experimental techniques and methods, virtual reality and accelerated sampling of rare hazardous scenarios, numerical research methods, and virtual simulation data collection.
6 Injury Biomechanics: Theories and Methods

Injury mechanisms and tolerance, injury risk assessment methods, human surrogates in automotive safety, injury protection in multiple fields, and the concepts and applications of active human response.
7 Safety Protection for Different Population Groups

Child occupant protection, occupant restraint system design, pedestrian protection, and related discussions and review.
8 Case Studies and Discussion

Case studies of severe high-speed rear-end collisions and battery safety, with an extended introduction to passive safety in formula racing.
9 Automotive Safety Testing and Evaluation

Test scenarios and tools, regulations and consumer-oriented assessment methods, safety testing under the four transformations of the automotive industry, and battery safety evaluation.
10 Machine Learning Methods and Applications in Automotive Safety

Fundamental concepts of artificial intelligence, commonly used machine learning algorithms, applications of machine learning in automotive safety, and related discussion and review.
11 Future Crash Scenarios and Risk Quantification

Future road traffic crash scenarios, intelligent vehicle risk prediction, prediction-based active hood systems, autonomous vehicle decision-making with ethical intervention, and safety and trust.
